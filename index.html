KEEP IT FAIR

Competition Rules

01. Content & Device Guidelines

Only 100% original photos/reels. No limit on entries; shoot on Phone, DSLR, or Drone. Basic editing/color grading is allowed, but fully AI-generated or scene-altering edits are not permitted.

02. Shooting & Respect Guidelines

Capture the Aagman moment, Sthapna, decorations & festive vibes. Do not capture Bappa's face before the Aagman. Keep all content respectful and culturally appropriate.

03. Submission & Instagram Profile

Register on our website and share your entry by collaborating with our Instagram page. Keep your Instagram profile public until winners are announced.

04. Verification & Drone Safety

Original files/EXIF details may be requested for verification. Follow local drone regulations; avoid flying over dense crowds or disrupting the procession.

05. Judging & Usage Rights

Judging is based on Composition, Storytelling, Festive Mood & Visual Quality — not likes or followers. Organizers may reshare entries on official pages with due credit.

13. Registration Deadline

22nd September, 11:59 PM. No entries will be accepted after the deadline.
